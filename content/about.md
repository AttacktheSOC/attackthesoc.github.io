## 👋 Hello!
I'm Dylan. I've spent about a decade in security, most of it buried in the Microsoft stack — Entra ID, Defender XDR, Sentinel, and more Azure Automation duct tape than I'd care to admit (some of it I'm genuinely proud of).

Lately I've moved toward the messier, more interesting end of the job: **DFIR and M365 forensics** — reconstructing what actually happened inside a tenant after things go sideways, and being able to prove it. Alongside that I've been going deep on what I've started calling **AI SOC engineering**: wiring LLMs into the repetitive parts of detection and response so analysts can spend their attention on the calls that actually need a human.

Most of what I work out ends up here — usually written up after I learned it the hard way.
